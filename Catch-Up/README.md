# 📝 Catch Up

## AI-Powered Meeting Intelligence

### **Don't replay the meeting. Understand what happened.**

Meetings generate enormous amounts of information, but the most important outcomes are often buried inside transcripts and notes.

**Catch Up** transforms meeting conversations into structured, actionable intelligence.

---

# 🎯 The Problem

A typical meeting can contain:

- Important decisions
- Multiple action items
- Risks and blockers
- Dependencies
- Follow-up discussions
- Ownership changes
- Priorities

But discovering these insights after the meeting often means going back through a long transcript.

Catch Up is designed to answer:

> **"What do I actually need to know from this meeting?"**

---

# 🧠 How It Works

Catch Up uses a multi-step reasoning workflow to progressively transform a meeting transcript into actionable context.

```text
Meeting Transcript
        │
        ▼
   Understand Context
        │
        ▼
   Extract Key Signals
        │
        ▼
     Identify
   Decisions & Actions
        │
        ▼
    Detect Risks
        │
        ▼
    Prioritize
        │
        ▼
  Actionable Summary
```

---

# 📌 What It Surfaces

### Decisions
What was agreed upon?

### Action Items
What needs to happen next?

### Risks
What could affect delivery or execution?

### Priorities
What requires attention?

### Follow-ups
What needs additional discussion or action?

---

# 👤 Persona-Aware Catch Up

Different roles may need different information from the same meeting.

### Product Manager
Focuses on decisions, customer impact, milestones, and blockers.

### AI Engineer
Focuses on technical decisions, engineering actions, dependencies, and risks.

### SRE
Focuses on operational risks, incidents, production dependencies, and reliability actions.

The meeting doesn't change.

**The context does.**

---

# 💡 The Core Idea

Catch Up isn't simply a meeting summarizer.

It explores the transition from:

**Transcript → Summary**

to:

**Transcript → Context → Decisions → Actions**
